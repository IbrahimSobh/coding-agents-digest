### Antigravity CLI
- ⚠️ **Google apps need their own approval** (1.4.9). Approving a Google site such as `www.google.com` no longer also allows Gmail, Docs, Drive or Cloud Console, so approve each app you need separately. [Source](https://github.com/google-antigravity/antigravity-cli/releases/tag/1.4.9)
- **Paste images into the prompt** (1.4.9). Paste an image with your terminal's paste key, such as `Cmd+V`, or drag image files into the prompt to attach them; this also works over SSH with X11 or Wayland forwarding. [Source](https://github.com/google-antigravity/antigravity-cli/releases/tag/1.4.9)
- **Stricter plugin name check** (1.4.9). `agy plugin validate` warns when a plugin name is not lowercase kebab-case, suggests a valid name, and fails under `--strict`. [Source](https://github.com/google-antigravity/antigravity-cli/releases/tag/1.4.9)
